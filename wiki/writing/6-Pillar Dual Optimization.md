# 6-Pillar Dual Optimization

## Summary

The six-pillar model is the writing frame for blog posts that need reader usefulness, Google quality alignment, and AI citation readiness.

This hub defines the skeleton. Later spoke notes should fill examples, checklists, and scoring rubrics.

## Pillar map

- Pillar 1: Intent and audience fit.
- Pillar 2: Original value and information gain.
- Pillar 3: E-E-A-T and trust signals.
- Pillar 4: Structure, headings, and answer-first flow.
- Pillar 5: Evidence, sources, and passage citability.
- Pillar 6: Technical publishing hygiene and measurement.

## Current fact anchors

- Google helpful content guidance, retrieved 2026-07-06, is the writing baseline for people-first content.
- The QRG revision in the substrate is dated 2025-09-11, with no newer revision recorded as of 2026-07-06.

## Drafting sequence

- Start from a brief in [[SERP-Informed Briefs and Outlines]].
- Confirm the reader problem.
- Choose the primary answer and supporting answers.
- Draft section openings as direct answers.
- Add evidence and caveats.
- Add internal links to [[Semantic Topic Clusters]].
- Add schema requirements from [[Blog Schema Stack]].
- Score with [[Blog Quality Score]] before handoff.
