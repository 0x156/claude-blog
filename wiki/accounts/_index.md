# Accounts Index

Account notes are intentionally sparse in this public vault. Do not store account names, credentials, tokens, emails, or private client data.

## Rules

- Use labels such as `property_a` in exported data.
- Keep account-specific details outside this vault unless sanitized.
- Route access decisions through [[Credential Boundary Rules]].
